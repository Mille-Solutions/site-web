# Privacy Policy
*Last updated: 19 November 2026*

Mille Solutions respects the privacy of personal data collected through the website millesolutions.mc, in accordance with Monaco Law No. 1.565 of 3 December 2024 on the protection of personal data.

### 1. Data Controller
Charlotte CHARLES MILLE, operating as a sole proprietor under the trading name Mille Solutions.

9 avenue d'Ostende, 98000 Monaco

### 2. Personal Data Collected and Purpose
When you use our contact form, we collect your name, email address, organisation name (if provided) and message.

This information is used solely to respond to your enquiry and manage any related correspondence.

The legal basis for processing is the taking of steps at your request prior to entering into a contract or, for other enquiries, our legitimate interest in responding to them.

Required fields are identified in the contact form.

### 3. Data Retention and Recipients
Enquiries that do not lead to a business engagement are retained for 12 months following the last correspondence.

Where a contractual relationship is established, relevant data is retained for the periods required by the contract and applicable legal obligations.

Personal data may be accessed by Mille Solutions and the technical service providers necessary for its processing, including Microsoft Forms for contact submissions and SMASH for professional email services.

The use of Microsoft Forms may involve data processing outside Monaco, subject to the applicable safeguards and service conditions.

### 4. Website Operation and Cookies
Technical data may be processed to ensure the proper functioning and security of the website.

Any non-essential cookies or tracking technologies are subject to applicable information and consent requirements.

### 5. Your Rights
Under applicable data protection legislation, you have rights including access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing and objection, as well as data portability where the relevant conditions are met.

To exercise your rights, you may contact Mille Solutions by post at the address provided above.

You may also lodge a complaint with Monaco's Personal Data Protection Authority (APDP): [https://apdp.mc](https://apdp.mc).

### 6. Updates to This Policy
This policy may be updated to reflect changes to the website, the services used or applicable legal requirements.
